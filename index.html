National Library

Near Post Office, Maharajganj, Azamgarh (UP) – 276137

National Library Maharajganj ki pehli library hai, jahan UP Police, SSC, Railway, Bank, TET aur board exam ki taiyari karne wale students ko shaant, anushaasit aur adhunik padhai ka mahaul milta hai.

Hamara maksad

Gaon aur kasbe ke har mehnati student ko shehar jaisi suvidha apne hi ilaake mein dena, taaki taiyari ke liye bahar na jaana pade. Yahan padhai ke saath discipline, regular attendance aur digital tools ek hi jagah milte hain.

FAQ

Fee kitni hai?

₹400 per month. 6 mahine ke liye ₹2400.

Library kab khulti hai?

Roz subah 7 baje se raat 9 baje tak. Holiday ki jaankari notice board par milti hai.

Admission kaise lein?

Students → Register mein form bharein. Manager approval ke baad login ho jata hai.

Seat kaise chunein?

Registration ke time khali seats dikhti hain, jo seat pasand ho us par tap karein.
